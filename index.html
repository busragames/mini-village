The World Tree is sleeping

Deep in the forest stands an ancient tree that has not woken for a thousand years. A small group of elves has come to live beside it, and you lead them.

Build a village along the river: plant berry groves, build treehouses and gather moonlight at night. When 20 elves live in your grove, plant the World Tree in the sacred glade and wake the forest.

The World Tree awakens

Golden light runs through its roots and the whole forest wakes up. Your elves are home.
